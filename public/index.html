How to play

Tap nearby dirt to dig. Tap farther away to walk; arrival does not dig. Your soft target circle shows reach. Daylight reaches 10 rows; better lights at the hub reach deeper. Broken blocks give each helper cargo. Carry it home to sell. Full holds spill ore for anyone to collect. Buy your next tool for more damage, or mobility for reach and speed. Granite needs a pickaxe. Detectors reveal where ore and oil hide. Hints are shared with your crew. Chip in to the team fund, vote a drill site and saving goals, then board the oil-powered plasma drill. Coolant opens the core. Fund the World Breaker to restore the planet.
